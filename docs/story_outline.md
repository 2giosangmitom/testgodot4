# Story outline (short — full script in docs/story.md, written by main session)

Nam, 20, second-year student in Hanoi, works night shifts at "Tiện Lợi 24h Ánh Dương" and rents room P.204
in a cramped dorm at the end of a winding alley. Roommate: Hùng. Classmate: Linh. Guard at school: bác Tư.
Store manager: chị Hạnh. At the dead end of the alley is an abandoned shrine (miếu) under a banyan tree,
next to an old sodium streetlight that "keeps watch". The shrine's oil lamp went out the night Nam moved in.

- Ch1 "Ca đêm đầu tiên": day at school (Scene 1) → night shift at the store (Scene 2): scan, stock, mop.
  The Gazer stands outside the glass in the fog, coming closer when unobserved. Walk home through the alley.
- Ch2 "Người gõ cửa": afternoon shift → night in the dorm (Scene 3): cook noodles, lock door.
  Phone messages say Hùng went back to his hometown. Someone with Hùng's voice knocks (Mimic).
  Opening/answering = death. Later "Linh" appears in the corridor too.
- Ch3 "Phố không người": the city at night is wrong. School street + courtyard (Scene 1, night fog 6–10 m)
  with the Crowd — frozen people that turn heads within 5 m and chase on noise. Collect items for the shrine
  (incense, old photo, lamp oil).
- Ch4 "Đèn đường cuối ngõ": the alley and the shrine (Scenes 2→3). Truth about the streetlight.
  Ending A "Bình minh": relight the shrine lamp with enough resolve → dawn.
  Ending B "Ca trực vĩnh viễn": Nam becomes the one who stands and watches under the streetlight.

Entity rules (gameplay):
- Gazer: moves only when not looked at (or during light flicker). Staring > 5 s → static → lose control → caught.
- Mimic: copies a friend's look/voice; neck tilted, arms below knees, casts no shadow. Check phone messages
  to know where the real person is. Never open the door / never answer.
- Crowd: statue-like groups (bus stop, sweeping). Within 5 m heads all turn to you. Noise (sprint, dropped items,
  flashlight) → they chase with stiff broken running. Walk slowly, keep distance.
